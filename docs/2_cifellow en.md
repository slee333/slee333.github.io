Welcome to the Clinical Informatics section! Clinical informatics is a broad field that utilizes information technology in medical practices to efficiently manage and analyze patient data, thereby improving healthcare services. I have a particular interest in the potential of wearables and digital healthcare, focusing on ways to efficiently manage everyday health through technology. After completing my internal medicine training, I am now a clinical informatics fellow at Mount Sinai in New York. In this section, I share experiences from the fellowship, hands-on topics like electronic health records and clinical decision support, and insights from the field. Join me on my journey through the fascinating intersection of healthcare and technology. 🤗🤗
