Welcome to the Clinical Informatics section! Clinical informatics is a broad field that utilizes information technology in medical practices to efficiently manage and analyze patient data, thereby improving healthcare services. I have a particular interest in the potential of wearables and digital healthcare, focusing on ways to efficiently manage everyday health through technology. After obtaining my internal medicine certification, I plan to apply for a fellowship in clinical informatics. In this section, I will share my preparation for the fellowship, the latest information, and insights from my experiences in the medical field. Join me on my journey through the fascinating intersection of healthcare and technology. 🤗🤗
